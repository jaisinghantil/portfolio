Research & Systems Design

ResQNow

A zone-based urban response infrastructure for Indian cities.

Problem

Indian cities do not lack services; they lack reliable response under urgency. Existing platforms are optimized for scale and volume, not predictable fulfillment within short time windows.

Core Insight

Urban reliability is not a function of scale, but of constraint. Limiting service radius and bundling heterogeneous tasks inside micro-zones can outperform city-wide platforms on reliability and economics.

System Architecture

Cities are divided into 1–1.5 km micro-zones. Each zone operates independently with local partners and a shared runner pool. Users raise tasks, not services.

Economic Logic

The model avoids inventory, dark stores, and subsidies. Simulation shows contribution-positive economics at approximately 35–40 tasks per zone per day.

Research Basis

Supported by comparative platform analysis, simulated demand modeling, and unit-economics benchmarking against hyperlocal and quick-commerce systems.

Limitations

Results are simulation-based, regulatory conditions vary by city, and scaling requires strong operational discipline.
